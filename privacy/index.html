Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information keepseeingnumbers.com ("we", "us") collects and how it is used.

Information you enter

Birth dates and names you type into our calculators are processed in your browser to calculate your numbers. We do not store them on our servers. If you start a report purchase, your name and birth date are saved in your browser's local storage only, so your report can be generated after payment.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Gumroad. We do not see or store your card details. Gumroad shares your email address and license key with us to deliver your report. See Gumroad's privacy policy.

Cookies and advertising

We use third-party vendors, including Google, that use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, through a Google-certified consent message.

Analytics and hosting

Our site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may process technical data such as IP addresses for security and performance. We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are visited.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

You can ask us about, correct or delete any information we hold about you by emailing hello@keepseeingnumbers.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
