Frequently asked questions

What are angel numbers?

Angel numbers are repeating number sequences, like 111, 444 or 11:11, that people notice again and again. In numerology each digit has its own meaning, and repetition is read as that meaning turned up.

How do I look up an angel number?

Type the number into the search box on this page. Times work too: type 11:11 or 1111. If we have a full guide, you go straight to it; if not, you get a quick digit-by-digit reading.

How are angel number meanings worked out?

Each digit from 0 to 9 has a traditional meaning in Pythagorean numerology. A sequence is read from its digits, how often they repeat, and its root number: the single digit you get by adding the digits together.

Are angel numbers real?

There is no scientific evidence that numbers carry messages. Many people use them as a prompt for reflection: a moment to pause and notice what they are thinking and feeling.
